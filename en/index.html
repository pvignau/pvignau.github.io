Pyve's place

The door creaks. Hi, I'm Pyve. Pierre-Yves Vignau, for the record.

A web developer by training, based south of Paris. My playground: front-end architecture, design systems and design tokens.

Since April 2026, I've been a software architect at France Télévisions, on a permanent contract. Before that, I was the design system's tech lead there, as a freelancer.

My inventory:

  • Design tokens: Style Dictionary, Tokens Studio, Terrazzo, the DTCG format.
  • Front end: TypeScript, React, Vue, Next.js, Storybook.
  • Figma: plugins and bridges to the code.
  • Home automation: Home Assistant, in Python.
  • AI: I pair-program with an agent.

My method: start from what exists, move in small steps, and write the docs, limits included.

The road so far:

  • Since 2026: software architect, France Télévisions (permanent).
  • 2023-2026: design system tech lead, France Télévisions (freelance).
  • 2014-2023: Infopro Digital, from web developer to head of frontend, by way of lead dev and front-end architect.
  • 2012-2014: web developer at agencies, PHP, Zend and Magento.

Off the clock: dad of two, into video games and two-wheelers.
